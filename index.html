Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy covers Kippi (the "App"), published by its developer ("we", "us"). By using the App you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. What we collect

Account information. Email address (or your Google/Apple account identifier, if you sign in that way). If you choose "Skip for now", we hold only a random account identifier until you attach a sign-in.

Purchase information. Whether you have an active subscription or trial, which plan, and when it renews or ends. This comes from Apple or Google through our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We never see or store your card details; Apple or Google handle payment.

Health and fitness data you provide. Sex, age, height, weight, activity level, dietary goals, and any weight entries you log over time. This is used to calculate your calorie and macro targets and to show you your history.

Food and workout logs. Text descriptions, photos of meals you choose to log, and workout entries (activity type, duration). Meal photos are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to identify food items and estimate nutrition; see §3.

Chat messages. If you use the chat assistant, your messages and the assistant's replies are stored so the assistant can see your history and give relevant answers.

Usage analytics. We use PostHog to understand how the App is used, for example which screens you open, whether a photo scan succeeded, how long onboarding takes. These events are tied to an anonymous or account-linked identifier, never to your name, email, or the content of your food photos or chat messages.

Device information. App version, OS version, device model, locale, and timezone, used for analytics and to display dates/units correctly.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or browsing history outside the App. We do not sell your data to anyone.

3. Third parties we use

Service Purpose What it sees
Supabase Database, authentication, file storage Your account and all data you log
Anthropic (Claude) Identifies food in photos you submit The photo and portion/nutrition output only, for that request
PostHog Product analytics Anonymized usage events (no PII, no photos, no free-text food/chat content)
RevenueCat Subscription and trial status across your devices Your account identifier, purchase and renewal history, your email if you gave one. No card details
Apple / Google Sign-in, if you choose that method; payment for subscriptions Your account identifier and your payment, per their own privacy terms

4. Your data, your control

  • You can edit or delete any food entry, workout, or weight log at any time in the App.
  • You can delete your whole account from the App: Profile > Delete account. Deletion is immediate and covers your account, every entry and photo, and the purchase record held by RevenueCat. It does not cancel a store subscription, which the App reminds you of first. Instructions, including for people who no longer have the App, are at aviv641999.github.io/kippi-legal/delete-account.
  • You can export a copy of your data by contacting us at shoptrendyline@gmail.com.

5. Data retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data immediately as described in §4. Aggregate usage analytics that were never tied to your name or email are kept.

6. Children

The App is intended for adults 18 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest (via Supabase's infrastructure). Access to your data is restricted to your own account via row-level security: no other user can read your entries.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. Material changes will be noted in the App or via email.
