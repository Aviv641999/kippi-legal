Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This policy covers Cru (the "App"), published by its developer ("we", "us"). By using the App you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. What we collect

Account information. Email address (or your Google/Apple account identifier, if you sign in that way).

Health and fitness data you provide. Sex, age, height, weight, activity level, dietary goals, and any weight entries you log over time. This is used to calculate your calorie and macro targets and to show you your history.

Food and workout logs. Text descriptions, photos of meals you choose to log, and workout entries (activity type, duration). Meal photos are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to identify food items and estimate nutrition — see §3.

Chat messages. If you use the chat assistant, your messages and the assistant's replies are stored so the assistant can see your history and give relevant answers.

Usage analytics. We use PostHog to understand how the App is used — e.g. which screens you open, whether a photo scan succeeded, how long onboarding takes. These events are tied to an anonymous or account-linked identifier, never to your name, email, or the content of your food photos or chat messages.

Device information. App version, OS version, device model, locale, and timezone — used for analytics and to display dates/units correctly.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or browsing history outside the App. We do not sell your data to anyone.

4. Your data, your control

  • You can edit or delete any food entry, workout, or weight log at any time in the App.
  • You can request full account deletion by contacting us at shoptrendyline@gmail.com — we will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.
  • You can export a copy of your data by contacting us at the same address.

5. Data retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data as described in §4.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest (via Supabase's infrastructure). Access to your data is restricted to your own account via row-level security — no other user can read your entries.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. Material changes will be noted in the App or via email.
