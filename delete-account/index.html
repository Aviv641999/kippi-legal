Delete your Kippi account

Last updated: September 15, 2026

You can delete your Kippi account and everything in it yourself, from inside the App, at any time.

From the App (immediate)

  1. Open Kippi and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. If you have an active subscription, the App shows its renewal date and offers to open your store's cancel screen first. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, because subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

What is deleted

Your account, profile and targets, every meal, workout, weigh-in and chat message, every meal photo, and the purchase record we hold with our subscription provider (RevenueCat). Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Anonymous usage analytics that were never linked to your name or email are kept in aggregate.

If you no longer have the App

Email shoptrendyline@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us the Google or Apple account you used) and ask for deletion. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Cancelling a subscription

  • iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Kippi > Cancel.
  • Android: Google Play > profile picture > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions > Kippi > Cancel.
